The Beatles released a new official music video for their 1965 classic “In My Life” on November 19, 2026. Starring young actor Owen Cooper, known for Adolescence, the film returns to the band's hometown of Liverpool to explore family memories, music and the ties between generations.

Event date: November 19, 2026 · Oldies Radyo | Music News

A new story for a 61-year-old song

Originally released on 1965's Rubber Soul, “In My Life” remains one of John Lennon's most personal reflections on people and places from the past. The song is back in the spotlight 61 years later through a new official video accompanying a contemporary mix of the original recording. It is not a newly written Beatles song, nor a band reunion.

According to the band's official announcement, the November 19 video stars award-winning actor Owen Cooper, known for Adolescence, and is directed by Jessica Swale and Irina Alexiu. Rather than relying solely on vintage footage, the filmmakers give the song a narrative about one Liverpool family across the decades.

A ukulele connecting generations

The film begins with a chance encounter in a Liverpool park in the 1940s. A treasured ukulele becomes part of a relationship and then moves through a family, connecting a mother and son and nurturing a love of music in later generations. As the story passes from post-war Liverpool through the 1960s to today, the song's themes of affection and remembrance take on new visual meaning.

Liverpool's landmarks are part of the story: the River Mersey, Royal Liver Building, Liverpool Cathedral, Royal Albert Dock and Strawberry Field all appear. References to the band's history include a playful nod to the famous Abbey Road cover and a moment recalling A Hard Day's Night. Liverpool Film Office confirms that the video was filmed around the city.

Rubber Soul gets another moment in the spotlight

The film arrives alongside the newly expanded Rubber Soul Special Edition. The Beatles' official site says Giles Martin and Sam Okell have prepared fresh stereo and Dolby Atmos mixes, with the original mono album, previously unreleased session recordings and demos also included. This new visual interpretation brings one of the record's most enduring songs to another generation of listeners.

The release also comes as Apple Corps develops a larger home for The Beatles Story in Liverpool, with an opening targeted for 2028 at the former St Johns Market. That attraction is a plan rather than an already opened museum.

From Oldies Radyo: When memories find a new audience

Some songs age without growing old. Every listener brings a different set of people and places to “In My Life,” and the new video illustrates that emotional continuity. A melody born in 1965 can still carry new memories in 2026 — an idea at the heart of Oldies Radyo.
