## Legal relay finding
A source station's bilateral agreement cannot expand its collecting-society licence. Geo scope, authorized service/operator/domain, apps, relay and listener reporting need explicit rightsholder authority. UK TuneIn appeal shows that even audio delivered directly by a source station can involve a separate communication issue: https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewca/civ/2021/441 (26 March 2021). GVL and Sena impose third-party/sublicensing restrictions.

## Current US rates caveat

It is not an unconditional final 2026 internet-only rate. Source: https://www.soundexchange.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2026-Commercial-Webcaster-Memo.pdf .
